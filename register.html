Who are you registering?

Kitchens that give food and organisations that receive it fill in different forms, because we need different details from each.

Donor (kitchen)

Restaurants, hotels, college and office canteens, banquet halls and caterers that have surplus food to give.

We ask for your kitchen's address and FSSAI licence number.

Recipient (shelter / NGO)

NGOs, night shelters, orphanages, old-age homes and community kitchens that collect and serve food.

We ask what food you accept, how many meals you can take each day and how far your staff can travel.
